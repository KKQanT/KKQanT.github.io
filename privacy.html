Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026.

The short version

gwork runs on your own computer. It talks to Google directly. There is no gwork server, so your data is never sent to us and we cannot see it, store it, or share it. Your sign-in token stays on your own disk in a file only you can read.

Who is responsible

gwork is maintained by Siametrics Consulting. For any question about this policy, write to peerakarn.j@siametrics.com.

What gwork accesses

When you authorise gwork, you grant it permission to act on your behalf in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Gmail, and to read your account's email address. It uses that permission only when you run a command, and only against the file or message that command names.

Concretely, the data involved is:

  • Your email address, so the tool can report which account is active.
  • The contents of files you point it at — a document you ask it to read, a spreadsheet you ask it to fill, a deck you ask it to build, a message you ask it to draft.
  • File metadata such as names, IDs and folders, when you list or search Drive.

gwork does not crawl your account, does not index your files, and does not read anything you have not asked it to act on.

Where that data is stored

File contents are not stored by gwork at all. They pass through your computer's memory while a command runs and are written only where you tell the tool to write them.

Two small files are kept on your own machine, both with permissions that make them readable only by your user account:

  • ~/.config/gwork/client.json — identifies the application to Google. It contains no personal information.
  • ~/.config/gwork/token.json — your personal sign-in token and your email address.

Neither file is transmitted anywhere. Neither is shared between users. Each person who uses gwork holds their own token, and every action is attributed to whoever signed in.

What is shared with third parties

Nothing. gwork has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no backend. The only network connections it makes are to Google's own API endpoints, to do the thing you asked.

Limited Use

gwork's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used solely to provide the features described on the home page, is never sold, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred to others except as required by law.

How to revoke access

Either of these ends gwork's access:

  • Remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This revokes the token immediately and everywhere.
  • Delete ~/.config/gwork/token.json on your machine. This signs that machine out.

There is nothing to delete on our side, because we hold nothing.

Children

gwork is a tool for professional use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the project's source repository.
